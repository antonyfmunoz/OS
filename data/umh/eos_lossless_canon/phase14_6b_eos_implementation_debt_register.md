| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| CRITICAL | Blocks production deployment or creates security vulnerability |
| HIGH | Prevents major feature area from working correctly |
| MEDIUM | Causes friction, maintenance burden, or quality degradation |
| LOW | Cosmetic, minor friction, or future-scaling concern |
