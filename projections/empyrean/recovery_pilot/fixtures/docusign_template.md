## 3. SERVICES

### 3.1 The Job Pipeline System

Provider will deliver The Job Pipeline System (the "**Services**"),
comprising:

**(a) Activation.** Configuration of a delivery environment for Client,
including message pipelines, booking calendars, and response handling
configured to Client's business hours, service area, and telephone number.

**(b) Pool import.** Import and validation of two contact pools from Client's
records: (i) past customers, and (ii) open estimates dated within the twelve
(12) months preceding the Effective Date.

**(c) Message library.** Preparation of message templates in Client's voice
for Client's review and approval. **No message is sent to any homeowner
without Client's prior written approval of that template.**

**(d) Outbound contact.** Contact of the imported pools on Client's behalf,
from Client's telephone number and business identity, in accordance with the
approved templates.

**(e) Response routing.** Routing of homeowner responses to Client and
notification of Client of each response.

**(f) Reporting.** A running ledger of contacts, responses, and outcomes,
accessible to Client, together with scheduled reviews under Section 3.2.

### 3.2 Reviews

Provider will hold reviews with Client at approximately day fourteen (14) and
day thirty (30) of the Guarantee Period, and at days sixty (60) and ninety
(90) of the Initial Term.

### 3.3 What the Services are not

The Services do not include performance of any roofing work, any warranty of
Client's workmanship, any representation to any homeowner regarding insurance
coverage, or any legal, insurance, or tax advice. **Client alone performs all
roofing work and is solely responsible for it.**

### 3.4 Compliance

Provider will configure the Services to honor opt-out requests, observe quiet
hours, and include required opt-out language in messages. **Client represents
that it has a lawful basis to contact each record it provides**, including
that the records were obtained through Client's own prior business
relationships. Client's obligation under this Section survives termination.

## 5. THE GUARANTEE

### 5.1 The Guarantee

Subject to Sections 5.2 through 5.6 and Section 6, Provider will produce
**between twenty (20) and twenty-five (25) Activated Qualified Opportunities**
within the thirty (30) day Guarantee Period.

### 5.2 Definition — "Activated Qualified Opportunity"

An "**Activated Qualified Opportunity**" means a single homeowner contact that
satisfies **all three** of the following, each evidenced in the ledger:

**(a) Two-way response.** The homeowner sent a substantive reply to a message
sent under the Services, or placed a return call that was connected. An
automated reply, a delivery receipt, an opt-out request, a wrong-number reply,
or a message that is not substantive **does not qualify**.

**(b) Agreed job type.** The homeowner's response concerns a job type on the
list of agreed job types at **Appendix B**, within Client's Declared Service
Area.

**(c) Ledger-evidenced.** The response is recorded in the ledger with a
timestamp, the contact reference, the message that produced it, and the
response content, and is available to Client for inspection at any time.

Each homeowner counts **once**, regardless of the number of responses.

### 5.3 What the Guarantee does not promise

**The Guarantee is a guarantee of Activated Qualified Opportunities. It is not
a guarantee of revenue, of booked jobs, of closed jobs, or of any dollar
amount.** Whether an opportunity becomes a job depends on Client's own sales
process, pricing, crew availability, and workmanship, all of which are outside
Provider's control. Any figures Provider has presented regarding potential
revenue are **estimates based on stated assumptions** and are not a promise.

### 5.4 Guarantee Period

The "**Guarantee Period**" is the thirty (30) consecutive days beginning when
Provider notifies Client that Activation is complete and outbound contact has
begun — **not** the Effective Date. The Guarantee Period is measured in
running days and is subject to tolling under Section 8.

### 5.5 Remedy if the Guarantee is not met

If, at the end of the Guarantee Period, fewer than twenty (20) Activated
Qualified Opportunities have been produced:

**(a)** Provider will **continue to perform the Services at no additional
fee** until the Guarantee is met;

**(b)** that continued performance is **capped at thirty (30) additional
days** (the "**Extension Period**"); and

**(c)** if the Guarantee is still not met at the end of the Extension Period,
**the Parties will revisit the engagement in good faith**, including
renegotiation, restructuring, or termination under Section 11.

During the Extension Period, Client's ongoing monthly fee obligation under
Section 4.1 continues unless the Parties agree otherwise in writing.
**[COUNSEL]**

### 5.6 Sole remedy **[COUNSEL]**

Section 5.5 states Provider's **entire obligation** and Client's **sole and
exclusive remedy** for failure to meet the Guarantee.

## 6. CONDITIONS PRECEDENT

### 6.1 The conditions

The Guarantee in Section 5 is expressly conditioned on Client providing, at
Activation, records meeting **both** thresholds:

| Condition | Threshold |
|---|---|
| Contactable past customers | **at least 200** |
| Open estimates dated within the last 12 months | **at least 50** |

### 6.2 Definitions

**(a) "Contactable"** means the record includes a telephone number or email
address that is valid and deliverable on validation. A record that fails
validation **does not count** toward the threshold.

**(b) "Open estimate"** means an estimate issued by Client, dated within the
twelve (12) months preceding Activation, that has not been won, lost,
withdrawn, or superseded.

**(c)** Counts are determined by Provider's validation of the records Client
actually provides, not by Client's estimate of them. Provider will report the
counts to Client in writing before the Guarantee Period begins.

### 6.3 Pro-rating below the thresholds

If Client's validated records fall below either threshold but are at or above
**one half** of both thresholds (that is, at least 100 contactable past
customers **and** at least 25 qualifying open estimates), the Guarantee is
**pro-rated** by the lesser of the two ratios:

| Validated past customers | Validated open estimates | Pro-rate ratio | Guarantee becomes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 200+ | 50+ | 1.00 | 20-25 |
| 180 | 45 | 0.90 | 18-22 |
| 160 | 40 | 0.80 | 16-20 |
| 140 | 35 | 0.70 | 14-17 |
| 120 | 30 | 0.60 | 12-15 |
| 100 | 25 | 0.50 | 10-12 |

The ratio is the **lesser** of (validated past customers ÷ 200) and
(validated open estimates ÷ 50). The pro-rated Guarantee is that ratio applied
to 20 and to 25, rounded down to whole opportunities, with a floor of one.

**Worked example.** Client provides 150 validated past customers (ratio 0.75)
and 45 validated open estimates (ratio 0.90). The lesser ratio is 0.75. The
pro-rated Guarantee is **15 to 18** Activated Qualified Opportunities.

### 6.4 Below one half of either threshold

If Client's validated records fall below one half of **either** threshold,
**no Guarantee applies.** Provider will notify Client in writing before the
Guarantee Period begins, and Client may elect, within five (5) business days:

**(a)** to proceed with the Services **without any Guarantee**, acknowledging
that election in writing; or

**(b)** to terminate this Agreement, in which case Provider will refund the
$5,000 payment less documented Activation work performed, **not to exceed
$1,500**. **[COUNSEL]**

### 6.5 Client's records

Client is responsible for the accuracy and completeness of the records it
provides. Provider is not liable for a shortfall in Activated Qualified
Opportunities to the extent it results from records that are inaccurate,
duplicated, out of date, or not lawfully contactable.

## 8. TOLLING

### 8.1 The clock pauses

The Guarantee Period **pauses** ("tolls") for the duration of any period in
which Provider is blocked from performing by:

**(a)** an approval under Section 7(a) outstanding **more than forty-eight
(48) hours** after request — tolling runs from the expiry of that 48-hour
window until the approval is given;

**(b)** access under Section 7(b) not provided by day three — tolling runs
from day three until access is provided;

**(c)** suspension for non-payment under Section 4.4;

**(d)** Client's written instruction to pause outbound contact; or

**(e)** a force majeure event under Section 14.

### 8.2 Tolling is logged

Every tolling event is **logged with a start timestamp and an end timestamp**
and is available to Client at any time. **Provider may not claim a tolling
event that is not in the log.** The Parties agree the log is the record of the
Guarantee Period's running length.

### 8.3 Effect

The Guarantee Period resumes when the blocking condition ends. Any day on
which tolling is in effect for any part of the day does not count toward the
thirty (30) days. Scheduled reviews move with the tolled clock.

### 8.4 Not tolling events

Weekends, public holidays, and ordinary variation in homeowner response rates
are **not** tolling events.

### 12.2 Sole-purpose use

Provider will use Client's data **solely** to deliver the Services to Client.

If the Parties do not proceed after Provider receives Client records — whether

systems.

### 12.5 Deletion on termination

On termination after Activation, Provider will delete Client records within

except for the ledger retained under Section 12.6.

### 12.6 Retention

Provider may retain the ledger and Guarantee records for **twenty-four (24)
months** for dispute resolution and its own records. Retained data remains
subject to Sections 12.2 and 12.3.

### 12.7 Security incident

Provider will notify Client **without undue delay and no later than
seventy-two (72) hours** after becoming aware of any unauthorized access to
Client data, describing what is known and what is being done. **[COUNSEL]**

**indefinitely**.

### 13.4 No other rights

Section 13 grants no rights beyond those stated. All other use requires
Client's prior written consent.
