**THIS SERVICES AGREEMENT** (the "Agreement") is entered into as of **[EFFECTIVE DATE]** by and between **Empyrean Creative LLC dba Empyrean Studios**, an Oregon limited liability company ("Provider"), and **[CLIENT LEGAL NAME]**, a(n) [STATE] [ENTITY TYPE] ("Client").

## 1. Services
Provider will implement and operate **The Job Pipeline System**: booked-job pipeline work across three sources — unanswered inbound calls, open estimates, and past customers — using Client's existing tools and phone number, with every outbound message pre-approved by Client (the "Services").

## 2. Fees
- **Activation fee:** $5,000, due at signing (covers activation and month one).
- **Monthly fee:** $2,500 per month thereafter, collected via Stripe subscription.
- **Initial term:** three (3) months, then month-to-month.
- Fees are flat. No percentage or per-job fees apply under this Agreement.

## 3. The Guarantee
3.1 Provider guarantees **20–25 Activated Qualified Opportunities** within thirty (30) days of Activation ("Guarantee Window"), subject to the Conditions Precedent (§4) and Cooperation (§5).
3.2 **"Activated Qualified Opportunity"** means a contact from an agreed pool who (a) makes a two-way response to outreach under this Agreement, (b) concerns an agreed job type within Client's declared service area, and (c) is evidenced by an entry in the attribution ledger maintained by Provider (§7).
3.3 If the guaranteed count is not reached in the Guarantee Window, Provider continues the Services **at no additional fee for up to thirty (30) additional days** (the "Extension Cap"). If the guaranteed count is still not reached at the Extension Cap, the parties will meet to revise scope, targets, or terminate under §11. **The Extension Cap is the entirety of the remedy for guarantee shortfall.**
3.4 The guarantee applies to Activated Qualified Opportunities only. **Provider guarantees no revenue, booked-job count, or close rate** — closing remains Client's responsibility.

## 4. Conditions Precedent
4.1 The full guarantee applies only when, at access: (a) Client's records contain **at least 200 contactable past customers**; and (b) **at least 50 open estimates dated within the preceding 12 months**.
4.2 **Pro-rating.** If Client is below either threshold but at or above one-half of both, the guaranteed count pro-rates by the lower ratio:

| Lower of (past customers ÷ 200, open estimates ÷ 50) | Guaranteed count |
|---|---|
| ≥ 1.00 | 20–25 |
| 0.75–0.99 | 15–18 |
| 0.50–0.74 | 10–12 |
| < 0.50 | No guarantee; Services proceed only by separate written election |

## 5. Cooperation; Tolling
5.1 Client will: approve or reject each message template within **48 hours** of delivery; provide read-only system access within **3 business days** of signing; confirm declared service area and job types at kickoff; and make a designated approver available ~10 minutes weekly.
5.2 **Tolling.** The Guarantee Window clock **pauses** during any period in which Client approval, access, or required cooperation is outstanding beyond the periods above, and resumes when cured. Provider will notify Client in writing when tolling begins and ends.

## 6. Exclusivity (by tier)
6.1 Client's **Declared Service Area** is the county/ZIP list in **Exhibit A**.
6.2 Exclusivity is tier-based: **Base tier** — no exclusivity; **Growth tier** — Provider will not serve a directly competing roofing company within [N]-mile radius of Client's primary office; **Partner tier** — Provider will not serve a directly competing roofing company anywhere within the Declared Service Area.
6.3 Provider will in all cases decline engagements that would require serving two direct competitors in the same market.

## 7. Attribution Ledger
Provider maintains a per-contact ledger (source pool, outreach history, responses, qualification, appointments, booked jobs, evidence references). The ledger is the **sole basis** for guarantee accounting. Client may dispute any entry; Provider will walk the evidence with Client within 24 hours and correct genuine errors.

## 8. Right of First Offer (Growth tier and above)
If, during the term and for twelve (12) months after, Client elects to pursue a sale of the business, Client will notify Provider and give Provider fifteen (15) business days to submit an offer before engaging other buyers. Client has no obligation to accept any Provider offer. This section imposes a first-offer procedure only — not a right of first refusal, option, or restraint on sale.

## 9. Data-Handling Addendum
9.1 Access is **read-only** and technically enforced where the platform permits.
9.2 Client data is used **solely** to perform the Services and produce Client's reports.
9.3 If the parties do not proceed past the audit, or upon termination, Provider deletes Client data within **7 days** and provides written confirmation.
9.4 A mutual NDA is available for execution before any access (standing link).
9.5 Provider processes homeowner contact data as Client's service provider, under Client's existing customer relationships; all outbound messages are sent from Client's number/identity with opt-out honored immediately and suppression lists maintained.

## 10. Anonymized Aggregate Data Rights
Provider may retain and use **anonymized, aggregated** performance data (e.g., response rates by pool, cycle times) for benchmarking and service improvement. Such data will never identify Client, its customers, or its pricing, and **will never be used to source, evaluate, or price any acquisition** of or investment in Client or any other business.

## 11. Term; Termination
Initial term three (3) months from Effective Date, then month-to-month; either party may terminate thereafter on fifteen (15) days' written notice. Either party may terminate for material breach uncured within ten (10) days of notice. §§8–10, 12 survive termination.

## 12. Limitation of Liability
Neither party is liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages. Provider's total liability under this Agreement is capped at the fees paid by Client in the three (3) months preceding the claim.

## 13. General
Independent contractors; no agency. Assignment only with written consent. Notices in writing to the addresses below. Entire agreement; amendments in writing. **Governing law: State of Oregon**; venue Multnomah County.
